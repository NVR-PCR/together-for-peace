About the Initiative

About Together for Tomorrow

Together for Tomorrow is an experiential learning initiative that introduces students to the values of peace, empathy, cooperation and responsible global citizenship. Through workshops, classroom activities, projects and competitions, the programme encourages students to become thoughtful individuals who contribute positively to their communities and the world around them.

Rather than a one-time event, the initiative weaves peace education into the rhythm of the school year — one workshop at a time, one project at a time, one act of kindness at a time.

Our Vision

To nurture compassionate, resilient and globally responsible citizens who contribute towards peaceful communities and a sustainable future.

Our Mission

To integrate peace education into everyday learning through meaningful experiences that inspire kindness, collaboration, critical thinking and responsible action.

Learning by Doing

Learning Projects

Four hands-on projects run alongside the workshops, turning ideas about peace and cooperation into everyday classroom practice.

1

Culture of Peace

Exploring peace through classroom discussions and collaborative learning.

Learning outcomes

  • Empathy and active listening in everyday conversations
  • Respectful communication across differences
  • Calmer, kinder ways of resolving disagreements
Expected duration: approx. 2–3 weeks Tentative
2

Co-operative Maths Games

Learning teamwork and cooperation through engaging mathematical activities.

Learning outcomes

  • Working together towards a shared goal
  • Mathematical confidence through play
  • Inclusive participation — everyone has a role
Expected duration: approx. 2–3 weeks Tentative
3

Kindness Graph

Encouraging and reflecting upon everyday acts of kindness within the school community.

Learning outcomes

  • Noticing and appreciating everyday kindness
  • Recording and reflecting on simple data
  • A warmer, more caring classroom culture
Expected duration: runs across the programme Tentative
4

Maths Across Cultures

Discovering mathematical ideas from different cultures to celebrate diversity and inclusion.

Learning outcomes

  • Appreciating contributions from many cultures
  • Curiosity about mathematical ideas around the world
  • Inclusion through shared discovery
Expected duration: approx. 2–3 weeks Tentative

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for students, parents and teachers.

Together for Tomorrow is the Peace Education initiative of Navrachana Higher Secondary School, Sama. It brings together workshops, classroom projects and competitions that help students explore peace, global citizenship and sustainable development through hands-on, experiential learning.

The programme is open to students of Navrachana Higher Secondary School, Sama. Workshops and projects run through regular class time, while the essay and research paper competitions welcome entries from participating students. The teacher–parent workshop invites families to join the conversation.
